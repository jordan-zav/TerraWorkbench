# Natural Resources Canada geophysical sample data notice

Source portal: <https://geophysical-data.canada.ca/portal>

The source portal states that its geophysical data are copyright His Majesty
the King in Right of Canada, 2024, and makes them available for commercial,
personal and public use. It permits reproduction in part or in whole, without
charge or further permission, subject to these conditions:

1. Keep the portal's terms and conditions with the data at all times.
2. Include the citation appropriate to the data type in any publication.
3. Notify the appropriate contact of any publication.

The portal refers users to the
[Open Government Licence - Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
Its dataset-specific conditions and disclaimer remain authoritative:
<https://geophysical-data.canada.ca/portal>.

Required citation for aeromagnetic, airborne electromagnetic, gravity and
radiometric survey or compilation data:

> Natural Resources Canada (2024), Canadian Airborne Geophysical Data Base,
> Airborne Geophysics Section, Geological Survey of Canada, Lands and Minerals
> Sector, Natural Resources Canada.

Publication notification/contact: <infogdc-infocdg@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca>

TerraWorkbench does not alter the ownership of these source datasets. Keep each
original metadata/readme file beside its corresponding grid, and record the
dataset's stable source URL when adding it to this directory.
