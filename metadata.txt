about=Provides QGIS Processing algorithms for magnetic, gravity and gamma-ray spectrometry data, including calibrated K/eU/eTh products, survey-specific radiometric corrections, GRS80, Bouguer and Airy reductions, IGRF-14, survey preparation, and optional SimPEG 3D inversion. Its dependency manager reads requirements/base.txt and keeps core 2D and optional 3D requirements separate in a TerraWorkbench-specific profile directory while preserving QGIS host-package precedence. TerraWorkbench is an independent community plugin.
