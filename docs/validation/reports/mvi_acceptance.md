# Downloaded references and three-geometry MVI acceptance

The 5 November 2026 campaign has three distinct evidence scopes. Public
downloaded observations test prediction through the Core API. Authored
synthetic vector bodies test geological characterization against known truth.
Private field-format checks are retained outside the repository and are not
used to certify geological recovery. No production numerical default changed.

The complete public record is
[mvi_acceptance_20261105.json](../records/mvi_acceptance_20261105.json).

## Downloaded public references

Observed data and topography come from the SimPEG user-tutorials repository,
pinned to commit d817cbf587605940d9125d4308a05c88610335e9. The registered files
are inv_gravity_anomaly_3d_files.tar.gz and
inv_magnetics_induced_3d_files.tar.gz. Their sizes are 10,984 and 10,797 bytes.
Archive and extracted-member SHA256 values are frozen in the source registry
and in the machine-readable record. Initial transfers were also checked
against the pinned repository's Git blob IDs. Cached data are verified and
reused; unexpected existing files are never silently overwritten.

Attribution: SimPEG contributors, including tutorial author Devin Cowan.
The pinned repository license is CC BY 4.0, recorded with its URL and hash.
See the original [gravity tutorial](https://simpeg.xyz/user-tutorials/inv-gravity-anomaly-3d/)
and [magnetics tutorial](https://simpeg.xyz/user-tutorials/inv-magnetics-induced-3d/).

Each downloaded dataset contains 289 observations and topography. Every fifth
station in file order is withheld before fitting: 231 training stations and
58 withheld stations. A declared coarse 20 m mesh, 160 m depth, three padding
cells and 40-step L2 budget produce 1,973 active cells. This is a bounded Core
prediction test, not replication of the tutorial's inversion mesh or recipe.
Withheld predictions use the Geoana engine and the same inducing field as
training; training predictions are cross-checked against Choclo.

The uncertainty is 2 percent of the peak observed anomaly. Gravity tutorial
prose says 2.5 percent, while its executable code uses 2 percent; this campaign
explicitly follows the code. Gravity uses upward-positive gz in mGal.
Magnetics uses nT, a 50,000 nT inducing field, inclination 90 degrees and
declination zero. The limits, fixed before fitting, are training WRMS at most
2 and withheld WRMS at most 3.

| Downloaded case | Training WRMS | Withheld WRMS | All audited inner solves | Prediction gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gravity | 0.927025 | 1.287638 | Pass | Pass |
| Induced susceptibility | 0.962313 | 1.583679 | Pass | Pass |

These downloaded archives do not include true-model arrays. This test does
not reconstruct truth from a separate forward notebook, so it makes no
geological-recovery claim for these two predictions.

## Three fixed vector-body cases

The shallow cube, deep cube and two-body geometry reuse the existing authored
[recovery fixtures](voxel_recovery_validation.md). Data are generated by
independent Gauss volume integration, with order-convergence and analytic
forward checks before inversion. These fixtures are not described as
downloaded field surveys. The two prisms have different magnetization
directions; they are separated by 200 m between their nearest faces.

The common mesh has 384 cells and 1,152 vector parameters. There are 81
training stations and 64 offset withheld stations, with independent noise
seeds and a fixed 0.3 nT standard deviation. The recipe uses the production
spherical API, 20 Cartesian initialization steps, at most 80 spherical steps,
20 IRLS cycles and CG 800 with relative tolerance 0.0001. It supplies no
true-body mask, support box, known direction or known-body coefficients.

The six body criteria are location, depth, volume, contrast, support and
direction. Existing thresholds are retained: location and depth at most
100 m, volume and contrast relative error at most 0.35, support Dice at least
0.6 and direction error at most 15 degrees. The legacy voxel-L2 criterion
of at most 0.5 remains scored separately; it is not relaxed or relabelled.

Depth is computed from six-face connected components at the declared support
threshold, using volume-weighted ENU centroids and minimum-distance matching.
Missing or mismatched component counts provide no valid depth estimate and
fail that criterion. A good anomaly fit cannot supply a missing body estimate.

| Authored case | Training WRMS | Withheld clean WRMS | Body checks passed | Voxel error | Inner precision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shallow cube | 1.004747 | 0.664842 | 1 / 6 | 93.7251% | Pass |
| Deep cube | 1.002372 | 0.680802 | 1 / 6 | 98.0823% | Pass |
| Two bodies | 1.031095 | 0.803987 | 0 / 6 | 96.1703% | Pass |

The only passing body criterion for the individual cubes is direction. The
predicted thresholded bodies fail the required characterization, including
component-count matching. All cases pass prediction and their audited raw
and spherical Jacobi-scaled inner tolerances. None passes geological acceptance.

Final stationarity is evaluated on the final frozen objective, using a fixed
Jacobi metric at the original spherical starting model. The declared criterion
is a factor-1000 projected-gradient reduction, with an absolute floor of
0.000000000001. Ratios are 0.188282, 0.933822 and 1.007602. None passes 0.001.
The SDK objective-change stop for the latter two cases is therefore not a
demonstration of this local coordinate stationarity condition.

This fixed unconstrained spherical recipe is not accepted as a general
geological recovery workflow. The result does not establish that every MVI
configuration fails or invalidate the successful scalar prediction tests.
The controls are retained without truth-based parameter tuning. See the
[outer-budget investigation](mvi_outer_sensitivity.md) for separate numerical
and prior-strength sensitivities.

## Field-format correction and reproduction

Read-only field inspection exposed sectioned whitespace files with a separate
slash before channel names and Tie_line markers. The Core detector now retains
those channel names, accepts spaced/tabbed Line/Tie variants and recognizes
UTMX/UTMY names with datum suffixes. Explicit projected X/Y pairs take precedence
over supplementary latitude/longitude channels. This assigns channel roles;
it does not infer or assign CRS, units or a vertical datum. Existing ordinary
table, quoting and slash behavior remain covered by regression tests.

The targeted suite passed 51 tests. All three numerical cases were repeated
after this text-only correction and their saved model/prediction arrays remain
exactly identical. Private field rows, acquisition metadata and file paths
are excluded from this public record.

Use scripts/fetch_acceptance_references.py with an explicit --cache directory
to verify or download the registered public inputs. Run
scripts/benchmark_downloaded_potential_fields.py with --cache and a fresh
--output for the two predictive tests. Run scripts/benchmark_mvi_acceptance.py
with a fresh --output for the authored vector-body campaign. Scientific
readiness is a reported verdict, separate from successful script completion.
